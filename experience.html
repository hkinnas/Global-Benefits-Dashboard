Sample-size caveat

Q4 2025 survey n=29; Q1 2026 n=53. The Q4 100% "more present at work" result was driven by 29 respondents and is unlikely to be a real population effect. The Q1 numbers with n=53 are more directionally trustworthy but still small — treat the trend lines as signals to discuss with Wellthy, not headline outcomes for an executive audience yet.
