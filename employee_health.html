⚠ Health Span Trend Alert — Declining Help-Seeking Behavior Q4 2025

EMEA EAP standout: Portugal

Portugal YTD util 38.2% vs 4.2% BoB — representing >90% of all EMEA cases. This is likely an onsite-clinic attribution effect rather than pure organic demand. Removing Portugal would likely put EMEA utilization at or below benchmark. This distinction matters for program design decisions.

EMEA plan design — mental health coverage

EMEA insurance program managed via MMB (23 policies, $68.9M premium). $310.6K in cost savings/avoidance YTD. Q1 FY26 benefit case ops: 1,300 cases (-32% QoQ). Car Program Support cases have highest escalation rate (33%) and longest avg age (10.6 days) — not a direct health issue but operational burden may affect employee experience.

EMEA wellbeing score: Romania (2/10) & Saudi Arabia Darwin (3/10)

MMB SLA survey: Romania scored 2/10 (active remediation plan). Saudi Arabia Darwin portal 3/10 (down from 7.6 annual avg). These low service quality scores may indirectly impact employee health outcomes if employees cannot efficiently access benefits.

Burnout risk signals — APJC vacation utilization (FY25)

Vacation utilization = leave taken / entitlement. Low utilization flags potential burnout and always-on culture. Japan (28%), Taiwan (30%), and Korea (36%) are concerning. India's elevated sick leave (71%) may reflect a different dynamic.

Korea — mental health coverage gap

Korea is the only country across all 14 APJC markets where mental health is explicitly excluded from insurance coverage. Korea EAP utilization not separately reported, but this plan design gap creates a structural barrier to mental health support for 297 employees. Wellbeing satisfaction is N/A (not collected). This should be a priority remediation item.

Japan & Taiwan — vacation under-utilization

Japan: 28% vacation utilization with no sick leave data available in source. Japan wellbeing satisfaction 9.5/10 is paradoxically strong — may reflect cultural reporting norms rather than true wellbeing. Taiwan: 30% vacation. Korea: 36% vacation. These three markets warrant a targeted culture conversation and policy review to reduce burnout risk.

Health Span Gaps & Priority Watch Items

P1 Gap — Korea: No Mental Health Insurance Coverage

297 employees (Korea) have zero mental health insurance coverage. This is the only such gap in the entire APJC portfolio. Action: Engage Korea benefits team and local insurer to add mental health as covered benefit by FY27 plan renewal.

P2 Risk — Japan/Taiwan/Korea Vacation Under-Utilization

28%, 30%, 36% vacation utilization in Japan, Taiwan, Korea respectively. Cultural norms contribute but this creates structural burnout risk. Action: Partner with HR teams in these markets on culture/manager enablement programs. Cross-reference with employee engagement survey data.

P3 Signal — Wellthy NPS & Outcomes Decline Q1 2026

NPS dropped 93 → 81 and "more present at work" fell from 100% to 33%. Small sample size means this may not be statistically meaningful, but requires a QBR conversation with Wellthy to rule out systemic issues. Action: Request Q2 2026 survey data immediately.

P4 Risk — Canada EAP Under-Utilization

Canada is the only region below BoB benchmark at 1.63% vs 2.5%. Given strong RethinkCare penetration (7.80%), awareness isn't the issue. Investigate counseling access routes, stigma, or Canada-specific EAP program design. Action: Deep-dive with Optum Canada team on access barriers.
