Reconciliation policy

Every per-EE adoption metric on this tab divides by the Cisco regional HC from the May 2026 snapshot, not the vendor's self-reported eligible base. The vendor number is preserved as a footnote so anyone comparing this dashboard to the underlying QBR can see the variance — usually small (Optum runs ~3-4% lower HC because of point-in-time vs. May snapshot). Wellthy and RethinkCare run materially smaller eligible bases (~60% and ~5% of full HC respectively) by program design, which the variance footnote makes explicit. The APJC country-team mart (preventive care above, well-being and time-off on the Experience tab) is rendered side-by-side with vendor data — it is a different system of record (country HR teams) and is not double-counted in headline KPIs.
