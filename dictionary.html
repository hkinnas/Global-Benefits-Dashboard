Locked policy decisions

Headcount denominator

Cisco Headcount (May 2026, 85,639 EE across 91 countries) is the canonical denominator for every per-EE metric in both Financials and Adoption. Vendor-reported eligible HC shown as a footnote variance only.

Shared global program attribution

Vendor lines funded inside the US Fringe budget that deliver value globally (Wellthy, Global EAP via UBH/Optum, ISOS, Pavelka, Bright Horizons National, etc.) are reallocated to all regions in proportion to Cisco HC share. Headline US tile shows both gross and post-allocation Cost/EE for transparency.

Currency normalization

USD is canonical. CAD converted at the monthly snapshot rate (currently 0.73). FX table stored in dashboard_build/inputs/fx_snapshot.csv and versioned with the build.

Side-by-side reconciliation (APJC + Budget Committed)

When two sources scope the same metric differently — APJC country-team vs ZBB, or PO-able vs payroll-fringe budget — the dashboard renders both, surfaces the variance, and keeps the long-running source (ZBB / FY26 Plan) as system of record. No silent overwrites.
