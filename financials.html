APJC country drill-in — new FY26 country-team data

Side-by-side, not replacement

The APJC Total Rewards team supplies a country-level FY26 plan view (% of payroll, per-EE USD) for 14 markets. We render it alongside the ZBB-derived APJC totals rather than replacing them — the country-team derived totals run 42–86% lower than ZBB allocated USD for the 12 countries with both views, almost certainly because the two sources scope payroll taxes and statutory mandates differently. ZBB stays the system of record for headline KPIs. Vietnam ($65), Taiwan ($552), and India ($990) per-EE values are verify-flagged as likely unit/scope errors and are not auto-corrected.

Budget Committed % — US/Canada PO vs FY26 Plan

Why the % looks low

US 11.1% / Canada 4.8% / combined 10.8% reflects PO Amt / FY26 Plan as of May 2026. The denominator includes ~$1.4B of US payroll-fringe lines (Medicare, social-security, employer payroll taxes) that are not PO-able — they flow through payroll and never appear as a Purchase Order. A more useful operational read is the line count: 88/181 (48.6%) of vendor-managed budget lines have a PO opened against them.
